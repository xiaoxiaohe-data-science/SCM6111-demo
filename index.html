Overview of data-driven forecasting

Historical demand pattern and next-week forecast

Seasonality + promotion effect

The forecasting logic uses weekday seasonality from the historical data set. A promotion on Thursday significantly increases demand, so the model adjusts expected sales before estimating ingredient ordering requirements.

  • Demand pattern is highly repetitive by weekday.
  • Friday and Saturday are peak turnover days.
  • Thursday promotion creates a clear demand uplift.
  • Holdings are planned only when ordering cost savings outweigh storage cost.
